Watch Ads is now a dedicated page.
Dashboard -> Watch Ads & Earn -> Watch Ads page.

The page shows:
- Daily ads completed / daily limit
- Visual progress ring and progress bar
- Ads remaining today
- GPX earned today
- GPX reward per ad
- Withdrawal progress for required ads and tasks
- How the rewarded ad flow works

The Watch button is the only place that starts a rewarded ad.
